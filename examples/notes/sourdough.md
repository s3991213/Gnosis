# Sourdough loaf — what finally worked

## Recipe (one loaf)
- 400g bread flour, 100g wholemeal
- 375g water (75% hydration)
- 100g active starter
- 10g salt

## Process
1. Mix flour and water, rest 1 hour (autolyse).
2. Add starter and salt, squeeze through until combined.
3. 4 sets of stretch-and-folds, 30 minutes apart.
4. Bulk ferment until ~50% bigger — about 5 hours at 24°C, longer if the kitchen is cold.
5. Shape, then cold-proof in the fridge overnight.
6. Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250°C: 20 min lid on, 20–25 min lid off.

## Lessons
- Flat loaves were under-fermentation, not a weak starter. Watch the dough, not the clock.
- Starter is ready when it doubles within 4–6 hours of feeding and passes the float test.
