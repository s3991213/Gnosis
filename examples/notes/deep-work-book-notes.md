# Book notes: Deep Work (Cal Newport)

**Core idea:** the ability to focus without distraction on cognitively demanding work is
becoming rarer and more valuable at the same time.

## Takeaways
- Shallow work (email, meetings, admin) feels productive but rarely creates new value.
- Schedule deep work in fixed blocks; willpower runs out, routines don't.
- "Embrace boredom": if you reach for your phone every idle moment, you train your brain to
  need novelty, and long focus gets harder.
- Shut down completely at the end of the day with a ritual — review tasks, plan tomorrow,
  then stop thinking about work.

## Things I want to try
- Two 90-minute focus blocks every morning, notifications off.
- Batch email to 11am and 4pm.
- Keep a tally of deep hours per week to see if it actually changes.
